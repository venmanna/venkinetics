Install VenKinetics

VenKinetics is your personal installable Progressive Web App (PWA). You get full offline gym functionality, smooth haptic feedback, and instant full-screen access directly from your phone's home screen.

On iPhone & iPad (Safari):

  1. Tap the Share button at bottom center of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  3. Confirm by tapping Add in the top right.

On Android (Chrome):

  1. Tap the three dots menu (⋮) in Chrome.
  2. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
